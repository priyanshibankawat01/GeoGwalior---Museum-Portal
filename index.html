ABOUT THE MUSEUM

Where Earth's history comes alive.

The GSI Geoscience Museum in Gwalior is a dedicated space for exploring the geological history of our planet.

Housed in the historic Victoria Market Building, the museum brings together geological specimens, fossils, minerals, interactive exhibits and stories about the evolution of life on Earth.

The museum was inaugurated in December 2024 and was developed by the Geological Survey of India to encourage public interest and learning in geoscience.
